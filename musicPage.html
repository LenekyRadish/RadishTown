Welcome to the music page!

Music feels very personal to me, but I thought it might be nice to share some of the songs I listen to.

Albums On Repeat

I'm honestly a hater of the playlistification of music, so here are a few albums I really enjoy!

A Matter of Time - Laufey

I got to see Laufey (pronounced lay-vey) perform this in concert last year, and it was absolutely amazing. I probably cried 2-3 times across the course of the album.

Highlighted Song: A Cautionary Tale

Tags: Jazz, Sad Girl Music, Orchestral

To Love in the 21st Centrury: the epilouge - Lyn Lapid

This album presents a linear storyline of the course of a situationship in modern times, presenting yet another reason why the playlistification of music sucks! Anyway, it's peak.

Highlighted Song: East Side

Tags: Whimsical, Sad Girl Music

Scatterbrain - emei

This album is amazing to listen to while doing mundane tasks because of how loud it is. It's loud in a good way though. It makes me feel very cool, even though I most certainly am not.

Highlighted Song: Cynical

Tags: Angsty

HOT POT! - Mikayla Geier

This artist is so underrated. This album in particular is very weird, but once again in a good way.

Highlighted Song: Pearl of Glue

Tags: Whimsical, Weird
